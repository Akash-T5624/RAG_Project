# HOU-END-2023 (endorsement version 2023)

In force 2023-01-01 to 2024-12-31. SYNTHETIC wording for the capstone.

<a id="h-exc-4-2"></a>
## H-Exc-4.2 Flood

Loss or damage caused by flood is excluded unless the Flood Extension is shown in the schedule.

<a id="h-exc-4-5"></a>
## H-Exc-4.5 Gradual escape of water

Damage caused by gradual or repeated escape of water over time is excluded.

<a id="h-exc-4-8"></a>
## H-Exc-4.8 Unoccupied property

Loss or damage while the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days is excluded.

<a id="h-exc-4-9"></a>
## H-Exc-4.9 Subsidence

Subsidence, heave or landslip is excluded unless caused by an insured peril.

<a id="h-ded-2-1"></a>
## H-Ded-2.1 Deductible

The deductible shown in the schedule applies to each claim. It is separate from any sub-limit and from the policy limit.

<a id="h-sub-1-3"></a>
## H-Sub-1.3 Sub-limits and policy limit

Sub-limits for named perils (flood, theft) are shown in the schedule and cap that peril only. The policy limit is the overall maximum for the period of insurance.
