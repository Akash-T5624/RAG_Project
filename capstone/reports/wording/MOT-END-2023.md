# MOT-END-2023 (endorsement version 2023)

In force 2023-01-01 to 2024-12-31. SYNTHETIC wording for the capstone.

<a id="exc-1-1"></a>
## Exc-1.1 Period of insurance

Loss or damage occurring outside the period of insurance shown in the schedule is excluded.

<a id="exc-2-2"></a>
## Exc-2.2 Unlicensed driver

Loss or damage arising while the vehicle is driven by a person who does not hold a valid driving licence is excluded.

<a id="exc-2-4"></a>
## Exc-2.4 Unpermitted driver

Loss or damage arising while the vehicle is driven by a person not on the permitted-driver list, without the policyholder's consent, is excluded.

<a id="exc-3-3"></a>
## Exc-3.3 Commercial use

Use of the vehicle for the commercial carriage of passengers for hire or reward, including ride-hailing, is excluded.

<a id="exc-4-2"></a>
## Exc-4.2 Goods for hire

Conveying goods for hire beyond the limits stated in the schedule is excluded.

<a id="exc-5-1"></a>
## Exc-5.1 Intoxicated driver

Loss or damage arising while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs is excluded.

<a id="exc-6-1"></a>
## Exc-6.1 Racing and speed trials

Loss or damage during racing, speed testing or reliability trials, including on a closed track, is excluded.

<a id="exc-7-4"></a>
## Exc-7.4 Flood and water ingress

Engine damage caused by water ingress is excluded unless the Engine Protector add-on is shown in the schedule. Other flood damage to the vehicle is subject to the flood sub-limit in the schedule.

<a id="exc-7-6"></a>
## Exc-7.6 Undeclared aftermarket accessories

Theft of non-OEM accessories not declared in advance is excluded; declared accessories are subject to the accessories sub-limit.

<a id="exc-8-1"></a>
## Exc-8.1 Tyres and tubes

Damage to tyres and tubes is excluded unless the vehicle is damaged at the same time in an accident.

<a id="ded-2-1"></a>
## Ded-2.1 Deductible

The compulsory deductible shown in the schedule is borne by the insured on each own-damage claim. It is separate from any sub-limit and from the policy limit.

<a id="sub-1-3"></a>
## Sub-1.3 Sub-limits and policy limit

Sub-limits for named perils (flood, theft of accessories) are shown in the schedule and cap that peril only. The policy limit is the overall maximum for the period of insurance.
